# #007 Baseball Simi · Simi Beisbolista

*Swings at every pitch, tips his cap at every strikeout and always says 'next time.'*

**Tier:** Common (map) · **Rarity:** Uncommon · **Height:** 12 in (30 cm), cleats on · **Weight:** 11 oz (310 g), glove included

**How to meet him:** Roams the map. Uncommon. Check parks and ballfields on sunny afternoons.

## SimiDex entry

A good-natured Simi found at ballfields on sunny days. He coaches, cheers and passes the sunscreen before the first pitch. He has never once complained about an umpire.

## Dialogue

- **Appears:** "Play ball! Got your cap? Got your sunscreen?"
- **Checked in:** "Safe! Checked in! Now reapply, it's a day game."
- **Missed toss:** "Strike one! Shake it off, you've got two more."
