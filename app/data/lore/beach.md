# #008 Beach Simi · Simi Playero

*Floral shirt, palm hat, huaraches. Reapplies sunscreen every two hours, on the dot.*

**Tier:** Shelf (QR) · **Rarity:** Rare · **Height:** 12 in (30 cm), 14 in with palm hat · **Weight:** 9 oz (255 g), sand not included

## SimiDex entry

A relaxed Simi who lives by the sun-care shelf. He is calm about everything except SPF. He always carries a spare bottle of sunscreen in his hat.
