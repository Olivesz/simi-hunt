# #016 Día de Muertos Simi · Simi Calaverita

*Painted calavera smile, arms full of marigolds. Here to help families remember.*

**Tier:** Event · **Rarity:** Legendary · **Height:** 12 in (30 cm), 14 in with marigold crown · **Weight:** 9 oz (255 g), plus a basket of pan de muerto

**How to meet him:** Event Simi. Appears on the map during Día de Muertos, November 1 to 2.

## SimiDex entry

A gentle event Simi who appears for Día de Muertos. He helps families build ofrendas and loves hearing their stories. Marigold petals mark the places he has visited.

## Dialogue

- **Appears:** "Follow the marigolds... ah, there you are. Come, tell me about your family."
- **Checked in:** "Checked in! Light a candle, share a story. ¡Recordar también es cuidar!"
- **Missed toss:** "No hurry. Tonight is for taking our time."
