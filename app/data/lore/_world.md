# The World of Simi Hunt

## One doctor, many outfits

There is only one Dr. Simi. He just owns a lot of clothes.

Dr. Simi goes wherever people need a little care, and he dresses for the occasion. At the soccer field he wears the green jersey. At the beach he wears the floral shirt and the palm hat. On a cold night he wears three scarves. Each look is a Simi, and every Simi will tell you the same thing with a straight face: *"Soy el mismo, nomás con otro sombrero."* Same Simi, different hat.

That is the whole idea of the brand, folded into one joke. Farmacias Similares built its name on *lo mismo, pero más barato*: the same care, for less. The Simis apply the rule to fashion. Same doctor, same heart, new outfit, and the hug is still free.

## The tone

- **Warm.** Every Simi is glad to see you. Nobody is ever scolded.
- **Funny.** Gentle jokes, puns in two languages, a mustache that refuses to be hidden.
- **Bilingual.** Simis switch between English and Spanish the way families do at the dinner table. Spanish lines are short and easy to follow from context.
- **Family-friendly.** Safe for a grandmother, a ten-year-old and the cashier.
- **Pro-health, never preachy.** Simis cheer for everyday habits: drink water, wear sunscreen, sleep, move, wash your hands, ask your pharmacist. They never promise to fix, treat or cure anything. They are a nudge and a smile, not a prescription.

## Where Simis live

Simis wander the neighborhood on their own errands. Fútbol Simi is at the park, Chef Simi is near the food trucks, and Sleepy Simi only comes out after dark. Wherever you walk, a Simi is somewhere close, running late for something.

Their real home is the **SimiStop**, a participating CVS. Each Simi has a favorite shelf and hangs around it like a regular at a café. Beach Simi naps by the sunscreen. Spa Simi has strong opinions about the skincare aisle. Astronaut Simi orbits the vitamins, slowly. A few Simis are so attached to one store that they never leave it at all, which is why you will only meet Texas Simi on Guadalupe Street.

Each shelf sign carries a small QR code. That code is the Simi's front door. Scan it and the Simi steps out to say hello, and the app shows you the real products on that shelf.

## Checking in, not catching

You never capture a Simi. You **check in** with one, the way a good doctor checks in with a patient: *¿cómo estás?*

The tool for the job is the **SimiCápsula**, a two-tone vitamin capsule, Similares blue on one half and white on the other. Toss it gently toward a Simi. It pops open, the Simi waves, poses for a photo, and stamps himself into your **SimiDex**. Then he goes right back to what he was doing. Nobody is trapped. The capsule is an appointment card, and the SimiDex is a scrapbook of everyone who said hi.

Simis are used to being tossed. Fans have been throwing Dr. Simi plushies onto concert stages for years, so a capsule toss is basically a high five. If a toss misses, the Simi just laughs and says *"¡Otra vez, campeón!"*

## The community

Everyone who plays belongs to the **SimiColonia**, the neighborhood of people who look out for each other. Players check in, collect, compare SimiDex pages and trade tips on which SimiStop has which shelf.

## Running jokes

1. **"¿Ya tomaste agua?"** Every Simi, no matter the outfit, eventually asks if you have had water today. The astronaut asks. The luchador asks mid-match. Santa asks on Christmas Eve. It is the one question no Simi can resist.
2. **The mustache always wins.** Helmet, mask, beard, cowboy hat: the white mustache shows, every time. It is non-negotiable.
3. **Same Simi, different hat.** Ask any Simi if he is a different Simi and he will say no, he just changed clothes. The SimiDex lists eighteen of him anyway.
4. **¡Cuídate!** Simis never say goodbye. They say *take care*, and they mean it.

## House rules for writing a Simi

- Every Simi is Dr. Simi: kind, a little vain about the mustache, quick with a hug.
- Outfits drawn from real cultures, such as mariachi, Día de Muertos and lucha libre, are written with pride and care. They celebrate traditions; they never make fun of them.
- Health lines stay general: water, sun, sleep, movement, clean hands, a talk with the pharmacist.
- No politics, no rivals, no villains. The worst thing that happens in this world is a missed capsule.
