# #017 Luchador Simi · Simi Luchador

*Mask on, cape flying, mustache poking out. A técnico through and through.*

**Tier:** Event · **Rarity:** Rare · **Height:** 12 in (30 cm), cape fluttering · **Weight:** 11 oz (310 g), 13 oz with the cape

**How to meet him:** Event Simi. Appears on the map on weekends, every Saturday and Sunday.

## SimiDex entry

A weekend event Simi known in the ring as El Bigote de Plata. He always plays fair and always helps his opponent up. His mustache is the only part of his face anyone has seen.

## Dialogue
