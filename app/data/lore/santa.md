# #018 Santa Simi · Simi Clos

*Red suit, big sack, and a beard that is mostly mustache. Brings care, not coal.*

**Tier:** Event · **Rarity:** Legendary · **Height:** 12 in (30 cm), 15 in with hat · **Weight:** 13 oz (370 g), after the cookies

**How to meet him:** Event Simi. Appears on the map during the holidays, December 1 to 25.

## SimiDex entry

A holiday event Simi who appears every December. His list has no naughty side and his sack has something for everyone. Experts agree the beard is mostly mustache.
