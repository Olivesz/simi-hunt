# #001 Classic Simi · Simi Clásico

*The original lab coat and mustache. Arms always open, hugs always free.*

**Tier:** Common (map) · **Rarity:** Common · **Height:** 12 in (30 cm), mustache included · **Weight:** 9 oz (255 g) of pure good vibes

**How to meet him:** Roams the map everywhere. The easiest Simi to find, and usually the first one to find you.

## SimiDex entry

The Simi that started it all. Found in every neighborhood at every hour, he greets players with open arms and a question about their day. Researchers note that all seventeen other Simis share his mustache.

- **Checked in:** "Checked in! Now, the important question: ¿ya tomaste agua?"
- **Missed toss:** "¡Otra vez, campeón! I have all day. Well, until lunch."
