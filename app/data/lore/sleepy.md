# #003 Sleepy Simi · Simi Dormilón

*Shows up after 9 p.m. to remind you that it is after 9 p.m.*

**Tier:** Common (map) · **Rarity:** Uncommon · **Height:** 11 in (28 cm), slouched · **Weight:** 8 oz (225 g), plus pillow

**How to meet him:** Comes out at night. Look for him after 9 p.m., when everyone else should be in bed.

## SimiDex entry

A drowsy Simi that appears only at night. Players who check in with him report a sudden urge to yawn. He is still searching for his other slipper.

## Dialogue

- **Appears:** "*yaaawn* Oh, hi. Shouldn't you be in bed? ...Shouldn't I?"
- **Checked in:** "Checked in. Now put the phone down. Buenas noches."
- **Missed toss:** "Mmm? Oh, you missed. That's okay. Five more minutes..."
