How it's measured

  1. Each signed QR claim is a scan of a real printed sign. Shelf signs carry a cryptographically signed code bound to one store and one shelf, so a claim can't be forged. With the pilot's location check on, it also means a visit near the store. Visits here are a proxy: sign scans, counted once per scanner per day.
  2. Coupon codes close the loop at the register. Unlocking a Simi reveals shelf products and a coupon; redemption at checkout ties engagement to a sale.
  3. Privacy-light. Counts come from claims and coupon codes only, with no personal profile required.
